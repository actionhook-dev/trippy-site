Frequently asked

Why does the app start with only three tabs?

Trippy starts in a Private View to protect you from accidental disclosure. Go to Settings and triple-tap the eye icon at the top to switch between Private View and full features.

Where is my data stored?

Everything is stored locally on your device in iOS’s standard sandboxed storage. There is no cloud sync. Deleting the app deletes the data. To move data between devices, use the JSON export in Settings → Journal Data.

Can I sync between devices?

Not automatically. Use the manual JSON export/import flow in Settings → Journal Data.

How do I report a bug or suggest a feature?

Send us an email with the details. We read everything.

Is this medical advice?

No. Trippy is a private journal and a harm-reduction reference tool. It is not a substitute for medical advice. Always consult a healthcare professional.
