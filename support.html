Frequently asked

What is Private View?

Private View protects you from accidental disclosure: it hides the Knowledge Base tab and substance details, so Trippy shows three tabs instead of four and reveals less at a glance. To turn it on or off, open Settings and triple-tap the pill at the bottom of the screen.

Where is my data stored?

Everything is stored locally on your device in iOS’s standard sandboxed storage, and your device backups (iCloud Backup or a computer) include it. With iCloud Sync on, your journal also syncs encrypted through your private iCloud database. Deleting the app deletes the data on the device; any iCloud Sync copy stays until you delete it in your iCloud storage settings.

Can I sync between devices?

Yes, with iCloud Sync. It is optional and off by default: turn it on in Settings → iCloud → iCloud Sync. It needs an iCloud account and starts at the next launch. Your journal, mood data and a few settings then sync encrypted through your private iCloud database to your devices on the same Apple Account. Export Data and Import Data in Settings still move a journal as a file; before an import replaces your journal, Trippy keeps a copy of it that you can restore from Settings.

How do I report a bug or suggest a feature?

Send us an email with the details. We read everything.

Is this medical advice?

No. Trippy is a private journal and a harm-reduction reference tool. It is not a substitute for medical advice. Always consult a healthcare professional.
