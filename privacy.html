Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-10-05 · Trippy iOS app

This policy describes what data Trippy handles. The short version: Trippy does not collect or share any personal data. Everything you enter stays on your device unless you turn on iCloud Sync, which keeps it encrypted in your own private iCloud database.

1. What we collect

Nothing. Trippy has no servers of its own, no Trippy accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no third-party SDKs that send data anywhere. We do not see, store, or have any way to access your journal entries, mood data, ingestion log, calendar history, or notes, with iCloud Sync on or off.

2. What stays on your device

The app stores the following locally, in iOS’s standard sandboxed Core Data storage on your phone:

  • Experiences you create (timing, substance, dose, notes).
  • Mood entries.
  • Custom substances, units, and consumers you add.
  • Settings (e.g. whether Private View is on, onboarding completion flag).
  • Copies kept so you can undo a big change: the journal an import replaced, the journal from before the 1.9 update (deleted when iCloud Sync starts), the journal from before you turned on iCloud Sync, and the journal that Replace This iPhone with iCloud removed. Delete Everything deletes them.

This data is deleted from your device when you delete the app. Like any app’s data, it is part of your device backups (iCloud Backup or a computer) if you make them. If you create a manual JSON backup via the in-app export, that file goes wherever you choose to save it. Trippy itself does not upload backups anywhere.

3. iCloud Sync (optional)

iCloud Sync is off by default. When you turn it on in Settings, your journal (experiences, doses, notes, ratings, locations, friends, custom substances, units, colors and classifications), mood entries and tags, and four settings (the 24-hour timeline clock, Disable Mood Tracker, the Statistics Prescribed/Recreational filter and the Sober Streak selection) sync through your private iCloud database to your devices signed in to the same Apple Account. Every data field is stored with CloudKit field encryption; Apple can still see record metadata such as record types, creation and modification dates, and which records link to which. Approved interaction pairs, the app lock, Private View, every other setting and the copies above stay on each device. Turning sync off stops it at once and keeps your iCloud copy until you delete it in your iCloud storage settings; Delete Everything while sync is on also deletes it. Signing out of iCloud while sync is on removes the synced data from the device. We cannot read your private iCloud database.

4. Permissions

Trippy does not request access to your location, camera, microphone, photos, contacts, calendar, Health data, motion data, or other personal data. Face ID is used only for the optional app lock; iOS tells Trippy only whether you were recognized.

5. Tracking

Trippy does not track you across apps or websites. The NSPrivacyTracking value in the privacy manifest is false. The app does not use the IDFA or any other tracking identifier.

6. Third parties

The app may surface reference links to publicly available harm-reduction resources (for example PsychonautWiki, TripSit). Tapping those opens them in your browser. Trippy does not share any of your data with those sites; their privacy practices are their own.

7. Children

Trippy is rated 18+ and is intended for adults. It is not directed to children and we do not knowingly collect information about minors. On iOS 26 and later, setup asks iOS for your age range through Apple’s Declared Age Range; Trippy uses the answer only on your device to confirm you are 18 or older and does not store or send it.

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this URL with a new effective date.
